# Model Card — CIC-IDS2017 binary intrusion detector (protocol v1)

Generated by `scripts/render_m5.py`; performance figures are quoted from the Milestone 4 tables (`reports/MODEL_EVALUATION.md`), explanation figures from Milestone 5 (`reports/EXPLAINABILITY_ANALYSIS.md`). Research/portfolio artefact; **not a production IDS**.

## Model

- Selected model: `E1_primary__lgbm__99966b1299` — LightGBM binary classifier, parameters `{"n_estimators": 3000, "learning_rate": 0.05, "num_leaves": 63, "min_child_samples": 500}`, 162 trees (validation early stopping). File `artifacts/m4/models/E1_primary__lgbm__99966b1299.joblib`, SHA-256 `e9a45e3dbb14a752674860cda17489cf8fe0d49f511748f8f8e5e96c154c3172`.
- Inputs: 81 CICFlowMeter-improved flow statistics (`configs/feature_manifest_v1.json`, default set; no IPs, ports, timestamps or identifiers).
- Output: probability of attack; alert when score ≥ 0.027598 (chosen on validation for a 0.1 % FPR target).
- Target: 1 = successful attack; 0 = benign including `- Attempted` flows.

## Intended use

Studying detection and explanation behaviour on the CIC-IDS2017 benchmark under a leakage-aware protocol. Not for deployment, traffic monitoring or security decisions.

## Performance (Milestone 4, chronological test, frozen threshold)

Recall 0.99965, precision 0.98203, FPR 0.00580 (validation FPR 0.00087), PR-AUC 0.99990; TP 151,576, FP 2,773, FN 53. Per-label recall, novel-vector results and comparison experiments: `reports/MODEL_EVALUATION.md`. The 0.1 % FPR target is not met on test.

## Explanations (Milestone 5)

- Method: path-dependent TreeSHAP (LightGBM native, equal to shap 0.52.0 TreeExplainer), computed for every test row. **Output space: log-odds of attack**; additivity verified on all rows.
- Global: `RST Flag Count` carries 40 % of mean |SHAP|; top five: `RST Flag Count`, `Bwd PSH Flags`, `Flow Duration`, `Bwd Init Win Bytes`, `Bwd Packet Length Std`. Groups: flags 54 %, timing 14 %, response sizes 13 %, TCP window 7 %.
- Several influential features (victim TCP window, response sizes) plausibly encode the lab's victim servers and tools rather than transferable attack behaviour (hypothesis).

## Known false-positive pattern

2,395 of 2,773 test FPs are reply-direction flows of same-day attack host pairs (Thursday internal scan, Friday external scan/DDoS); such flows have test FPR 77.1 %, while all other benign flows have 0.080 %. They are short, reset-terminated flows that the model scores like scan traffic. They are "false positives under the benchmark labels"; whether they are attack traffic is a labelling question left open.

## Known failure cases

53 missed attacks (53 novel vectors): SSH-Patator 25 of 888, Portscan 14 of 47720, Infiltration 10 of 11, DoS Slowhttptest 1 of 522, DoS Slowloris 1 of 1158, Heartbleed 1 of 3, Infiltration - Portscan 1 of 21530. In 50 of them the absent reset flag is among the top contributions toward benign. Classes with 3–22 test rows have no reliable performance estimate.

## Uncertainty and stability

Explanation aggregates are stable under row resampling (minimum top-10 overlap 1.0 over 200 bootstrap samples of 1,000 rows), but a Random Forest trained on the same data attributes differently (top-10 overlap 0.5, rank Spearman 0.65). Refit variability was not measured.

## Limitations

- Explanations describe model behaviour, **not causal network-security mechanisms**.
- Within-scenario benchmark: train and test share tools, hosts and scripted runs; no claim of real-world or unseen-attack generalisation.
- Validation FPR does not transfer to the test period; see the explainability report §17.
- Protocol-v1 labels are used as given; no relabelling.
