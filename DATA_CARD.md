## Collection context (from the official page)

- Period: 09:00 Monday 3 July 2017 – 17:00 Friday 7 July 2017 (5 days).
- Testbed with "12 different machines in Victim-Network" (Windows, Ubuntu, Mac OS X) and modem, firewall, switches, routers; traffic captured via a mirror port.
- Benign background traffic from the B-Profile system: abstract behaviour of 25 users over HTTP, HTTPS, FTP, SSH and email.
- Flow features: "more than 80 network flow features" extracted with CICFlowMeter.
- Labelled flows are based on timestamp, source/destination IPs and ports, protocol and attack.

## Feature structure

- **Original `MachineLearningCSV.zip`:** not inspected (behind registration form). Schema **unverified**.
- **Improved version [3]** (verified on the downloaded files): 5 per-day CSVs (`monday.csv` … `friday.csv`), identical 91-column header, comprising
  - identifiers / metadata: `id`, `Flow ID`, `Src IP`, `Src Port`, `Dst IP`, `Dst Port`, `Protocol`, `Timestamp`
  - target-related: `Label`, `Attempted Category`
  - 81 CICFlowMeter flow statistics (packet counts/lengths, inter-arrival times, flag counts, bulk, subflow, window, active/idle, plus `ICMP Code`, `ICMP Type`, `Total TCP Flow Time`).
- Data types, missing/infinite values, anomalies: see GENERATED block and `reports/DATA_AUDIT.md` §2, §6, §9.

## Licence / access conditions

- CIC page: dataset is "publicly available for researchers"; users should cite [1]. No explicit redistribution permission is stated on the CIC-IDS2017 page.
- Improved version [3]: authors request citation of [2] (WTMC/SPW 2021 artefacts) and [3] (CNS 2022). **No licence text found** on the download page or the CNS2022_Code repository page — unresolved.
- Project policy: data are **not** committed to this repository; users download from the source.

## Known limitations and data-quality concerns (from primary sources)

- Engelen et al. [2]: problems in traffic generation, flow construction, feature extraction and labelling; >20 % of original traffic traces reconstructed or relabelled in their version; original CICFlowMeter terminated TCP flows after a single FIN and ignored RST.
- Liu et al. [3]: further errors in attack orchestration, feature generation, documentation and labelling; DoS Hulk described as mis-implemented; nearly all Hulk flows filterable by four `Total Length of Bwd Packets` values (a potential shortcut).
- Lanvin et al. [4]: packet misorder, packet duplication, and performed-but-unlabelled attacks.
- Structural: each attack type occurs in a single short time window on one day; attacks are scripted from a small set of attacker hosts (per labelling logic in [3], e.g. `172.16.0.1`). Consequences for identifier leakage and split design are analysed in `PROJECT_PLAN.md` §6–7.
- Synthetic testbed traffic from 2017; representativeness of modern networks is not established.

## Preprocessing performed

- CSV → Parquet (`data/interim/`), lossless; adds `source_file`, `source_row`, parsed `ts`. Raw files unchanged (hashes verified by tests).
- Milestone 3 (`scripts/prepare.py` → `data/processed/*_v1.parquet`): 5 rows with infinite rates removed (listed in `reports/generated/m3_removed_rows.csv`); protocol-0 and negative-header-length rows kept and flagged; no duplicates deleted.
- Label policy (per authors' guidance in [3]): "Attempted" flows are not a separate class; `y_binary` relabels them Benign; sensitivity variant excludes them. Multiclass `family` unifies `Infiltration - Portscan` with `Portscan`. Original `Label` and `Attempted Category` are preserved.
- Split and evaluation protocol: `reports/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md` (v1, frozen).

## Intended use

Offline benchmarking of flow-based intrusion-detection methods, leakage and evaluation-methodology research, explainability research.

## Inappropriate use

- Claiming production readiness or real-world detection rates from benchmark results.
- Using identifier fields (IPs, timestamps, flow IDs) as model features and reporting the result as detection performance.
- Treating labels as error-free ground truth.
