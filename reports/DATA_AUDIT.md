## 9. Other anomalies

- `Protocol` = 0: 1,510 rows ({'BENIGN': 1508, 'Portscan': 2}); most frequent Flow IDs {'8.6.0.1-8.0.6.4-0-0-0': 1184, '192.168.10.50-192.168.10.3-0-0-0': 153, '192.168.10.12-192.168.10.3-0-0-0': 57, '192.168.10.16-192.168.10.3-0-0-0': 54, '192.168.10.8-224.0.0.22-0-0-0': 18}. - Label taxonomy: `Portscan` (Friday) and `Infiltration - Portscan` (Thursday) share identical feature vectors (88,460 rows) — the same scanning behaviour under two names.
